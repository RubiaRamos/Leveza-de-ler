Frankenstein Or the Modern Prometheus

Sinopse

Mary Shelley’s Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus tells the tragic story of Victor Frankenstein, a Swiss scientist who successfully creates a living creature from scavenged body parts, only to abandon it in horror. The intelligent and gentle creature, repeatedly rejected by humanity due to its grotesque appearance, turns to malice and vows revenge on Victor. This leads to the systematic murder of Victor's loved ones, culminating in a relentless pursuit across the Arctic wastes that ends in mutual destruction.
